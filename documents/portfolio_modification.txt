PHASE 5 — PROJECTS PANEL SPECIFICATION
Objective

This is the most important page in the entire portfolio.

If a recruiter spends 80% of their time anywhere, it should be here.

Everything else (Home, Education, Experience) exists to answer who you are.

The Projects page answers:

"Can this person actually build software?"

This page should feel like a project showcase application, not a gallery.

Every interaction should encourage exploration.

Design Philosophy

The Projects page should not overwhelm visitors with information immediately.

Discover → Browse clean project tiles.

Design Philosophy

This page should feel like opening a collection of polished case studies, not scrolling through a list of repositories.

The interaction pattern is intentionally simple:

Browse → Click → Expand → Explore → Close

That single interaction model keeps the interface intuitive while giving you the freedom to showcase each project in as much technical depth as you want. It also scales naturally as you add new projects over the years—every new project is just another tile in the grid that follows the same structure.


PHASE 6 — CONTACT PANEL SPECIFICATION
Objective

The Contact panel should answer one question:

"How can someone reach you immediately?"

This page is not meant to be a traditional contact form with lots of fields. Since you're a developer showcasing a portfolio, the goal is to make it effortless for recruiters, hiring managers, or collaborators to contact you or view your professional profiles.

The page should feel like a clean final destination, encouraging action without unnecessary friction.

The Contact panel follows the same design language as the rest of the portfolio.

Design Philosophy

The Contact panel should be the cleanest page in the portfolio.

There's no need for elaborate layouts or long descriptions. By the time a recruiter reaches this panel, they've already seen your work. The final step should simply remove every obstacle between them and contacting you.

The overall interaction is:

Arrive → Choose a contact method → One click → Connect

That makes the experience feel effortless and leaves the portfolio with a polished, professional ending.
