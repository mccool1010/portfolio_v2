# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Hari Krishna for **AI/ML roles first, full-stack roles second**, remote or in India.

- **Recruiters** usually arrive from a shared link (résumé, LinkedIn, email) and skim on a phone or laptop between other candidates. Their job: confirm role fit, location/remote, and grab the résumé or email, in about 30 seconds.
- **Hiring managers and engineers** go deeper. Their job: judge whether the projects are real, end-to-end systems and whether the engineering holds up (stack, architecture, upstream contributions).

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio. A visitor should leave convinced of one thing: **Hari ships real end-to-end AI systems (data → model → real interface), not notebook demos.** Within ~30 seconds they should also be able to confirm role fit (AI/ML + full-stack, open to remote or India) and reach the résumé or email.

Success means the visitor contacts Hari, downloads the résumé, or forwards the link.

## Positioning

Every featured project is a working system with a real interface, and the project and open-source data syncs itself from GitHub every 6 hours. The site shows current, verifiable work rather than a static list of claims.

## Operating Context

- Live link already circulating with recruiters; changes ship through a `v3` preview branch on Vercel and only reach `main` after the owner approves.
- Three sliding tabs: **Work** (`/`), **Open source** (`/#open-source`), **Contact** (`/#contact`). Deep links and the back button work.
- Content lives in `data/profile.json` (hand-edited by the owner) and `data/github.json` (generated by `scripts/update-github.mjs` via GitHub Actions; never edited by hand).
- Visitors use both phones and desktops; phone width is a first-class check (top bar must fit, contact cards swipe).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: plain HTML/CSS/JS, **no framework, no build step**. `vercel.json` stays as is.
- Must keep: canvas 3D scenes (hero terrain, per-project scenes, contact globe), light/dark mode (follows local clock, manual toggle remembered), the three sliding tabs, the GitHub auto-sync.
- No stock photos and no photo of Hari.
- Facts in `data/profile.json` are fixed; no new claims. The PyTorch PR must be described as "opened a PR proposing a fix; maintainers resolved it through a test-infra workflow". It must never be described as merged.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Hari Krishna. Monogram "HK".
- Voice: plain, confident, first person, short sentences, no buzzwords (e.g. "Systems, not demos.", "builds things that think.", "Code in other people's projects."). Keep this voice.

## Evidence on Hand

- Six featured projects with real stacks and points in `data/profile.json` (SlipSense, SecureVision AI, AI Talking Tom, ML Improvement Platform, Agentic RAG Chatbot, Voice HR Bot); two have live demos.
- Open-source PRs: sktime #11236 (merged), PyTorch #198730 (opened a PR proposing a fix; maintainers resolved it through a test-infra workflow).
- ICPC 2026 Online Challenge rank, MediHack 2024 2nd place, NASA Space Apps 2024, Kaggle RSNA Knee score, internships, education, certifications, all in `data/profile.json`.
- `resume.pdf` at the site root.
- No testimonials, client logos, press or photos exist. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof over adjectives.** Every claim points at a working system, a repo, a PR or a number from `profile.json`.
2. **30-second fit check.** Role, availability, résumé and email are always one glance or one tap away, on every tab and at phone width.
3. **AI/ML leads, full-stack supports.** Order and emphasis favor the AI/ML work; full-stack shows the ability to ship the product around the model.
4. **Truthful and current.** Data stays synced from GitHub; wording never overstates an outcome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific standard was set. Default to WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard navigation of tabs, visible focus, readable contrast in both themes, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for the canvas scenes and tab slides.
